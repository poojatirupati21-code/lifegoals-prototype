# First-home reel: script + prompts for a photoreal AI-video version

The MP4 in this folder is a best-effort animated version. For a truly photoreal animated human
(like a 3D/AI-video character), use an image-to-video tool (Veo, Runway, Kling, Luma, HeyGen/Hedra for lip-sync)
with the supplied portrait as the reference. Use `voice2.wav` (narration) for lip-sync.
Only use a person's likeness with their consent.

## Character prompt (reuse in every shot)
"Photoreal 3D-animated character based on the reference portrait: man in his late 50s/60s, grey-black swept hair,
moustache, warm brown skin. Wearing a tailored charcoal-navy two-piece suit, crisp white shirt, burgundy silk tie.
Expressive, friendly, confident presenter. Cinematic soft lighting, shallow depth of field, vertical 9:16, 24fps."

## Shots (voice-over = one line per shot; see script2.py for exact text)
1. HOOK (0:00) – Close-up, he raises a palm: "Stop." Bold on-screen text: STOP BEFORE YOU SIGN.
2. STORY (0:10) – He stands outside a Dublin terraced house with a SOLD board, smiling, then worried; thought bubbles: "Can I afford it? What's the catch?"
3. TIP 1 budget – Desk, laptop, payslip. Overlay: 4× income, 10% deposit, "repayment you can sleep on".
4. TIP 2 costs – Stack of envelopes labelled stamp duty, legal, survey, insurance falls onto a deposit slab.
5. TIP 3 benefits – He opens a gift box: Help to Buy, First Home Scheme, Local Authority Home Loan, vacant-property grants.
6. TIP 4 options – Four doors/signs: Fixed, Variable, Green, Term length.
7. TIP 5 approval – Bank meeting; stamp lands: APPROVAL IN PRINCIPLE. Checklist: savings habits, credit record, debts.
8. TIP 6 house – Walks through a house with surveyor and solicitor; BER A–G strip.
9. TIP 7 protect – Rain on the roof, shield over the house, emergency-fund jar.
10. ROADMAP – Winding road, 8 milestones, ends at the keys.
11. PAYOFF – Door opens, keys in hand, family/confetti, "8 months later".
12. LIFEGOALS + CTA – Phone showing LifeMap + verified expert; Save / Share / Start your plan.

Disclaimer on last frame: Dramatised story. Rules and figures change; confirm current Central Bank, Revenue and scheme details. Not financial advice.
