## The problem
- The latest prototype (`Lifecast User Jouney Prototype.html`) asks too many questions: 12 in the opening Discover, then 10 separate assessments in the "Me / Understand Me" tab.
- The older `LifeGoal User Journey Mobile Prototype.html` is too cluttered and complex.
- Goal: take out anything that is too complex, overwhelming or likely to be disliked by an ordinary customer.

## Part 1 — Discover and "Me / Understand Me"
1. Look: follow the theme, colour palette, fonts and UI style of the latest prototype. Use the journeys of both prototypes and pick what works best for a layperson. Simple but attractive. Easy but catchy.
2. Opening Life Chapter (Discover) questions: cut to **6–7 only**, taking 1–2 at most from each life chapter.
   - **Do not change the wording of the Discover questions** that exist in the Life Chapter questions. Select from them; don't rewrite them.
3. The same answers must be **already filled in** when the customer later goes deeper in the "Me / Understand Me" page.
4. "Me / Understand Me" page: **12–15 questions maximum in total** (including the pre-filled Discover ones). Today it has groups like
   A) Who I am — What matters to me, Money mindset; B) How I act — Money habits; C) Risk & investing — Risk comfort, Capacity for loss, Financial resilience, etc.
   Replace the long list with a simpler structure, e.g. "Know your risk appetite, risk capacity, investment capacity…". Merge 2–3 questions into one where sensible. Short questions, short options.
   Cover what a proper fact-find needs (QFA / CFP practice: attitude to risk, capacity for loss, investment capacity, time horizon, knowledge & experience, sustainability preferences, emergency fund/resilience, etc.). Research this.
5. Whether to show the Discover questions at the very start as an engaging "glimpse": decide what is best (xlsx Step 1 puts the questionnaire in Discover).
6. If Discover stays at the start: **never ask for uploads there**. Invite the customer to go deeper / make it accurate later. Document upload or manual entry only comes once the main life-goal plan starts (xlsx Step 3 "Add your money once", after account + verification).

## Part 2 — only after Part 1 is right
7. **Timeline** (screenshot 4): this becomes the centre of **My Plan**. Customers drag and drop goals along the timeline; editing year and amount sits underneath and is only needed if they want to. Make it work well, like the mobile prototype's LifeMap drag.
8. **Your finances** page = upload documents OR type it in manually. Keep it simple, only:
   1. About you — name, age, retirement age
   2. Income and expenses
   3. Assets
   4. Liabilities
   5. Protection
   6. Pension
9. **Results** page:
   1. "Your goals" box — every goal they added, name + percentage, headed "How much of each goal your future income and savings can cover." (screenshot 5)
   2. "What if" — add or subtract a monthly contribution and see the effect.
   3. "Your future, at a glance" → **Journey** view: the green/amber curvy road with goal milestones and the retirement flag (screenshot 1).
   4. Same card, tabs for **Life chapters** (screenshot 2) and **Detail** cashflow chart (screenshot 3).
   5. At the end, three options: **Download my plan** (cashflow, detailed plan with explanations, life-chart style report), **Book my adviser meeting**, **Adjust my goals**.
