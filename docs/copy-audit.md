# Copy audit (journey-spec §20)

Copy pass on the customer-facing text of the LifeMap prototype and workbook, 6 October 2026. Sub-Agent A: content, tone, grammar, brand. Sub-Agent B: UX writing, brevity, scannability. Only the words changed: no code, markup, IDs, interpolation, logic or numbers.

## Summary

- **26 text blocks** reworded in the prototype (28 occurrences) and **4 README lines** in the workbook. All other copy was read and left alone.
- **Words:** the 31 changed strings went from 848 to 681 words (-19.7%). Across all 1,136 customer-facing strings of 30+ characters in the prototype, 12,074 words became 11,907 (-1.4%). The copy was already plain, so most of the saving is where a sentence was padded.
- **Banned words:** none of the ban list (delve, testament, revolutionize, foster, comprehensive, tapestry, furthermore, moreover, in conclusion, leverage, seamless, empower, unlock, navigate, journey-as-filler, "it's important to note") appears in customer-facing text. The only hits are 'journey' in the Plan tab name 'Journey' (a view name, not filler) and developer comments.
- **Kinds of edit:** accuracy fixes where §17 to §19 left old wording behind (usual range, "Enter 0", fixed goal order, workbook "Partner rates block"); passive to active; contractions; trimming restated headings; one run-on split into short sentences; one slang verb and one exclamation mark removed.
- **Numbers are identical:** calc-vs-xlsx and the full suite results are listed at the end.
