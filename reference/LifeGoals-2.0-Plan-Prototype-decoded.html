Help & common questions

Is this financial advice?
No. LifeGoals gives you guidance and a projection based on the information you provide. Personal advice happens in your meeting with a qualified advisor.
Do I have to buy anything?
Never. The plan is free and there's no obligation at any point — even after meeting an advisor.
Is my information safe?
Your data is encrypted and used only to build your plan. Documents you upload are read automatically and are not shared without your consent.
What if my numbers aren't exact?
That's fine — reasonable estimates are enough to see the big picture. Your advisor can refine everything later.
How are the projections calculated?
Your figures are run through a professional cashflow-modelling engine (the same one advisors use), with standard assumptions for inflation, investment growth and income growth. Every projection is illustrative, not a guarantee.
